MsgDrive 隱私權政策

最後更新:2026-11-04

MsgDrive 是個人使用的訊息與檔案瀏覽網頁,程式都在你的瀏覽器裡執行。它沒有後端伺服器,也沒有帳號系統。

我們不收集什麼

MsgDrive 不收集、不儲存、也不販售你的資料。網站沒有分析工具、廣告或 cookie,你的訊息、檔案和登入資訊不會傳到作者手上。

你的資料放在哪裡

資料只放在你自己選的地方:本機資料夾、Dropbox、Google Drive 或 pCloud。瀏覽器直接和你選的服務連線,中間沒有經過 MsgDrive 的伺服器。那些服務如何處理資料,以它們自己的隱私權政策為準。

Google 使用者資料

使用 Google Drive 時,MsgDrive 只要求 drive.file 權限,也就是只能存取由 MsgDrive 自己建立的檔案,看不到你 Google Drive 裡的其他檔案。它也會讀取你的 Google 帳號信箱和儲存空間用量,只用來在畫面上顯示和判斷,不會傳給任何人。

你貼上的 OAuth 用戶端 ID 與密鑰,除了存在你的瀏覽器,也會存一份到你自己的 Google Drive(資料夾裡的 .msgdrive/msgdrive-google-client.json),讓你的其他裝置不必再貼一次。這個檔案只在你的帳號裡,不會傳給作者或任何第三方;刪掉它不影響資料。

MsgDrive 使用和傳輸從 Google API 取得的資訊時,遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策,包括其中的「限制使用」規定。這些資訊不會傳給第三方、不會用於廣告,也不會用來訓練 AI 模型。

存在你瀏覽器裡的內容

為了能運作,瀏覽器裡會存登入資訊、設定,以及讀過的內容和尚未同步的修改的暫存。這些只在你的裝置上。清除這個網站的瀏覽資料就會全部移除。

撤銷存取權

隨時可以到各服務的設定撤銷 MsgDrive 的存取權。Google 帳戶請到第三方應用程式和服務。撤銷後,已存在雲端的檔案還在你的帳戶裡。

網站託管

網站放在 GitHub Pages。GitHub 可能為了安全和維運記錄訪客的 IP 位址,詳情見 GitHub 的隱私聲明。MsgDrive 拿不到這些紀錄。

聯絡方式

有問題請到網站 repo 的 Issues 回報。

MsgDrive Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

MsgDrive is a personal web app for browsing messages and files. All of it runs in your browser: it has no backend server and no account system.

What we do not collect

MsgDrive does not collect, store or sell your data. The site has no analytics, ads or cookies, and your messages, files and login details never reach the author.

Where your data is

Your data stays only where you choose to keep it: a local folder, Dropbox, Google Drive or pCloud. Your browser connects directly to the service you chose, with no MsgDrive server in between. Each service's own privacy policy governs how it handles your data.

Google user data

When you use Google Drive, MsgDrive requests only the drive.file permission, which covers only the files MsgDrive itself created; it cannot see the other files in your Google Drive. It also reads your Google account's email address and storage usage, only to display them and make decisions on screen, and sends them to no one.

The OAuth client ID and secret you paste are kept in your browser and also as a copy in your own Google Drive (.msgdrive/msgdrive-google-client.json inside the folder), so your other devices do not have to ask for them again. This file stays in your account and is never sent to the author or any third party; deleting it does not affect your data.

MsgDrive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This information is not transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

What your browser keeps

To work, the browser stores your login details and settings, along with a cache of what you have opened and any changes not yet synced. All of this stays on your device. Clearing this site's browsing data removes it.

Revoking access

You can revoke MsgDrive's access at any time in each service's settings. For a Google account, go to Third-party apps and services. Files already in the cloud stay in your account after you revoke access.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operations; see GitHub's privacy statement. MsgDrive has no access to those logs.

Contact

For questions, open an issue in the site repo's Issues.
